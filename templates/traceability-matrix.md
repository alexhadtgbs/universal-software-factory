|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Rules:
- Every in-scope requirement should map to a story or have a documented reason why not.
- Every story should have observable acceptance criteria.
- Important acceptance criteria should map to tests or documented manual verification.
- Release readiness should report uncovered requirements and failed or missing evidence.
