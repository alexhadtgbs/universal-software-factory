# Workflow 04 — Engineering Plan

1. Confirm the PRD and relevant PDR/ADRs are approved.
2. Decompose stories into small tasks with explicit dependencies.
3. Define files/modules likely to change, test approach, and acceptance evidence.
4. Identify security/privacy-sensitive actions and required approvals.
5. Assign work to a capable agent or human based on actual tool support and context.
6. Define completion conditions before execution.
7. Keep tasks scoped; stop and request a decision if implementation reveals a material product or architecture conflict.
8. Update status and traceability links as work progresses.

Output: engineering plan with task IDs, links, dependencies, ownership, verification, risk, and status.
