# Workflow 01 — Product Discovery

## Goal
Turn an initial idea into a validated product brief without prematurely choosing implementation technology.

## Steps
1. Restate the problem and intended outcome.
2. Identify user segments, primary persona, context of use, and current alternatives.
3. Ask targeted questions about pain points, urgency, success, constraints, budget, deadline, privacy, and distribution.
4. Separate known facts from assumptions and unanswered questions.
5. Define measurable outcomes and non-goals.
6. Identify the smallest useful release and risks that could invalidate the idea.
7. Produce `product/product-brief.md`.

## Deliverable template
- Product / working name
- Problem statement
- Target users and context
- Current alternatives and shortcomings
- Proposed value
- Goals and measurable success metrics
- Non-goals
- Constraints (confirmed vs assumed)
- Key user journeys
- Risks and unknowns
- Open questions and owner
- Recommendation: proceed, validate further, or stop

Do not select native macOS, web, microservices, or another technology solely from the initial idea. Record product-form-factor and architecture questions for the decision workflow.
